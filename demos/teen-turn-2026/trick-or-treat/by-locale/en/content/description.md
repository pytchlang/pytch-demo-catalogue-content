# Trick or treat!

In this Trick-or-Treating simulation, your character, who is dressed
in a vampire costume, goes around the neighbourhood and collects some
sweets of different rarities, before returning home to see what you
gathered and how rare each sweet is.

![Five-part map of game world](map.png)

I drew all five of the backgrounds and costumes for the sprites myself
on a digital art app called procreate.

![Drawing image assets with _procreate_](drawing-assets.jpg)

The design is just whatever came to me naturally as I was drawing, and
I didn’t have a solid plan except for the fact I wanted it to be very
simple to navigate and to see, while also being dark as if it was
night time.

I am most proud of the fact that I was able to code the transitions
between the backgrounds as the character travels through the
neighbourhood. This is what I expected to be the greatest challenge,
but, although difficult, was not my biggest obstacle. I also loved how
I fully customised it by drawing every part of it myself.

With more time, I could add an in-game currency that you earn from the
sweets you collect, where you can buy more costumes for the character,
and maybe pay for new neighbourhoods to explore. The higher value
costumes could maybe up your chances for rarer sweets, and new
neighbourhoods could unlock new varieties.
